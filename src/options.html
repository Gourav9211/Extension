Settings

Everything runs on this machine. Stockfish and Maia are bundled with the extension and no position is ever sent anywhere - only the Gemini explanation, and only if you add a key.

General

Live analysis
Master switch. When off, no board is watched and the extension stays idle until you turn it back on.
Board coordinates
Show file and rank labels on the chess.com board.
Sound notification
Short chime when a new best move is ready.

Engine

Analysis depth
Higher is stronger but slower. 22 is a good balance.
Engine move time (ms)
Hard cap on how long the engine may think per position.
Move time cap (ms)
Keeps the popup responsive on positions where the depth limit and the time limit disagree.
Multi-PV (lines)
How many candidate moves to score, so the popup can show runner-up options.

Auto-play

Play the recommended move
Clicks the move on the board for you.
Adapt to opponent rating
Reads the opponent's chess.com rating and adjusts: against lower-rated players it deviates more and replies a little faster.
Move timing
How long to wait before playing. "Match opponent" replies just faster than their measured pace.
Beat opponent by (ms)
How much faster to reply than the opponent's pace.
Max move time (ms)
Upper bound applied in every timing mode.
Think time min (ms)
Fastest a move is ever played.
Think time max (ms)
Slowest a normal move is played.
Slow moves - frequency (1 in N)
0 disables slow moves entirely.
Slow think time min (ms)
Lower bound for a deliberately slow move.
Slow think time max (ms)
Upper bound for a deliberately slow move.
Alternative moves - frequency (1 in N)
How often a quiet position gets a 2nd/3rd-best move instead of the engine's first choice. 0 disables this.
Quiet-position limit (± cp)
Alternative moves are only played when the evaluation is within this margin. Mates and critical positions always get the best move.

Conflict. "Play Maia's move in auto-play" is on in the Maia section. Maia's auto-play replaces this alternative-move logic entirely, so the settings below won't affect played moves.

Fair play. Auto-play clicks moves for you. Engine assistance and automation violate chess.com's rules and can get your account banned. This is for practice against the computer only.

Maia

Enable Maia
Predict the move a human of a given strength would actually play, mistakes included, instead of always taking the engine's first choice.
Model
The 3M model is faster and smaller; the 5M model plays noticeably better. Either covers every rating.
Player strength (Elo)
Any rating from 100 to 3000. It is fed to the network, not chosen from a list of models.
Opponent strength (Elo)
0 means "assume the opponent is equally strong", which is usually right when Maia plays both sides.
Play Maia's move in auto-play
Replaces the alternative-move logic above. The two are never combined.
Show Maia in the popup
Adds Maia's own move distribution to the popup.
Blunder guard (± cp)
A weak player hangs pieces. Any Maia move costing more than this against Stockfish is rejected and the safest alternative is played instead. 0 disables the guard.
Maia candidate moves
How many of Maia's top moves may be played. Higher values let it choose from more of its own preferences.
Temperature
How far Maia may wander from its favourite move. 1 follows Maia's own probabilities.
Engine lines for the guard
Extra Stockfish lines searched so Maia's candidates can be scored. Costs a little engine time.

Limits. Maia reads the position only: castling rights, en passant and the clocks are not part of its input, so it will not castle for safety or count moves in a time scramble. Fair play: any auto-play violates chess.com's rules and can get your account banned.
